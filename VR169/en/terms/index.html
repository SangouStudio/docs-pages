2. Using this product

  • This product is a 3D model with gimmicks, for VRChat avatars. It is a custom model that lets you operate VirtualLens2, and it has no photographic function of its own. What it does depends on the specifications and limits of VirtualLens2.
  • This product is designed to be operated in VR mode, using VR equipment.
  • To use this product with full-body tracking, you must set the tracking and IK / spine mode to "Head lock" or "Spine lock".
  • This product supports Humanoid Avatars 3.0 avatars only. Non-Humanoid avatars are not supported.
  • We cannot guarantee that it works on every avatar. It may not work correctly on avatars whose arms or legs are unusually short or unusually long.
  • By the nature of OSC communication, packet loss can occur depending on your network. The bundled application does not guarantee perfectly reliable synchronisation with VRChat.
  • While operating in VR equipment you may strike nearby objects, furniture or other people. Make sure you have enough space around you and remove anything your body could touch. Damage is handled as set out in the Terms of Use.
